# CDD-007 Review

## Principal Engineer

Pass. Domain evaluation, evidence validation, persistence, and currentness projection are separated and typed. Records are frozen dataclasses and database mutation is rejected by a trigger.

## Chief Architect

Pass. Knowledge consumes existing Assertion Records and Governance-produced Acceptance Evidence. It does not create approval, modify upstream cognitive records, or alter the CEO.

## Business Reviewer

Pass. Only Institutionalized evaluations establish Institutional Knowledge. Candidate and Rejected outcomes retain their distinct KRM-001 meanings.

## QA Reviewer

Pass subject to CI. Tests cover evidence validation, all outcomes, confidence, explanations, override, configuration validation, migration state, immutability trigger presence, and the canonical Knowledge bypass guard.

## Architecture drift

- No EAH, RFC, CEO, EAD, ERM, SRM, ASM, KRM, Logical Model, or Physical Model was modified by the implementation.
- No canonical entity, attribute, or relationship was introduced.
- Acceptance Evidence and Knowledge Evaluation Record are authorized BCS artifacts, not CEO additions.
- No architecture layer was bypassed and no technology was added.
