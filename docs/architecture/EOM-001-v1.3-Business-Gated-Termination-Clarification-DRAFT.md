## Current authoritative language

“Partial completion is an internal orchestration condition, not an externally visible execution state. If required capability coordination does not complete successfully, the external execution outcome is Failed under ESM-001.”

## Proposed replacement language

The Cognitive Engine exclusively determines execution completion and technical execution failure.

A **successful business-gated termination** occurs when a governed capability outcome or authorized pre-capability determination instructs orchestration not to invoke later capabilities. The invocation has completed its governed work and transitions to ESM `Completed`. The business result remains non-actionable or indeterminate as specified by its governing contract. No uninvoked capability is represented as completed.

A **technical partial execution** occurs when required coordination cannot continue because of validation, persistence, concurrency, adapter, or unexpected technical failure. It transitions to ESM `Failed`.

Successful business-gated termination and technical partial execution are distinct internal orchestration conditions. Neither introduces a new ESM execution state. Completion remains determined only by the Cognitive Engine.

Add RFC-014 and CIM-001 to traceability after approval.

## Reason

Business non-actionability is not technical failure. The clarification preserves ESM's four-state model while allowing outcome gates to terminate safely.

## Compatibility

Conditionally backward compatible. Executions previously classified `Failed` solely because a governed business gate stopped later capabilities will become `Completed`; technical failures remain unchanged. Consumers must continue to retrieve the business result rather than infer actionability from ESM state.
