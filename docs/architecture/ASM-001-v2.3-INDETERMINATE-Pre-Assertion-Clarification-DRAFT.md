## Current authoritative language

“Assertion Outcome — Established, Candidate or Rejected.”

“Established: Exactly one Subject–Predicate–Object proposition is accepted. Candidate: One or more candidate Assertion propositions may exist. Each candidate is represented by its own Assertion Record. Rejected: The rejected proposition is preserved together with its supporting evidence for audit purposes.”

## Proposed additive language

Before Assertion Evaluation begins, the invoking integration must demonstrate that the minimum governed evidence required by ASM is present and non-conflicting. For the RFC-014 supplier-risk vertical slice, missing or conflicting evidence produces the integration determination `INDETERMINATE` and stops before ASM invocation.

`INDETERMINATE` is not an Assertion Outcome, does not create an Assertion Record, and does not change the authorized outcomes Established, Candidate, or Rejected. It carries no canonical or institutional standing. ASM continues to require at least one Enterprise Entity Resolution Record and one Semantic Resolution Record referencing the Assertion subject.

Add RFC-014 and CIM-001 to traceability only after those artifacts become frozen and authoritative.

## Reason

This preserves ASM's closed outcome vocabulary while preventing missing or conflicting evidence from being misrepresented as Candidate, Rejected, or a technical failure.

## Compatibility

Backward compatible. Existing ASM records, services, persistence, and outcomes do not change. Integrations that do not use RFC-014 are unaffected.
