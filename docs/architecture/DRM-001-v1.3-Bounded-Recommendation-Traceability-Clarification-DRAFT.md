## Current authoritative language

DRM-001 requires a Decision Recommendation, Decision Outcome, Decision Explanation, Governing Policy Reference, Policy Version, and one or more Institutional Knowledge items. “Policy satisfaction is demonstrated through evaluation against one or more governed business policies referenced by the Decision Evaluation Record.”

## Proposed additive language

For the RFC-014 supplier-risk sourcing slice, Decision Explanation and supporting traceability must identify:

- policy identifier and policy version;
- the exact evaluated RFC-014 rule;
- relevant supplier-risk and sourcing-status inputs;
- supporting Institutional Knowledge and evidence references;
- resulting Decision Outcome; and
- one recommendation from CIM-001's closed vocabulary.

The recommendation vocabulary is bounded to this slice and does not replace DRM's general Decision Recommendation. Policy thresholds and classifications remain policy-supplied. `NO_AUTOMATED_RECOMMENDATION` records that no automated recommendation has standing; it does not execute an action or erase a prior immutable evaluation.

No new Decision Evaluation Record attribute is required. The traceability is represented through existing Governing Policy Reference, Policy Version, Supporting Knowledge References, Decision Explanation, Decision Outcome, and evidence references available through the knowledge/assertion chain.

## Reason

Adapters must transport policy results, not own or hard-code business decision semantics.

## Compatibility

Backward compatible for records outside this vertical slice. Existing DRM schema and outcomes remain unchanged. Implementations must validate the closed vocabulary only when RFC-014/CIM-001 contract version applies.
